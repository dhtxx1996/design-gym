# pH-Sensitive Protein Design Task

## Problem Statement

Design a pH-responsive protein based on PDB ID **5L33** that undergoes a conformational change or destabilization when transitioning from neutral pH (7.4) to mildly acidic conditions (~6.0).

## Requirements

Your solution should:

1. Implement a complete computational design pipeline
2. Validate your designs using structure prediction
3. Provide scientific justification for all design choices
4. Discuss the mechanism of pH-sensitivity and potential applications
